Shipping Policy

Last Updated: 11/09/2026

Thank you for shopping at SwiftSelect. We are committed to delivering your order safely and on time.

📦 Shipping Information:

  • All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.
  • We ship directly from our international suppliers to your doorstep.
  • Tracking information will be sent to your email once your order ships.

Estimated Delivery Time:

🇳🇬 Within Nigeria: 7 - 14 business days

🌍 International: 10 - 21 business days

Shipping Delays:

Please note that shipping times are estimates and may vary due to customs, postal delays, public holidays, or unforeseen circumstances. We are not responsible for delays caused by external factors.

Lost or Damaged Items:

If your item is lost in transit or arrives damaged, please contact us immediately at 08024300891. We will investigate and provide a replacement or refund where applicable.

📌 Important: Please ensure your delivery address is correct before confirming your order. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

For any shipping questions, contact our support team at 08024300891.

📜 Refund Policy

Last Updated: 11/09/2026

At SwiftSelect, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, you may request a refund within 14 days of receiving your order.

Eligibility for Refunds:

  • Item must be defective, damaged, or significantly different from the description.
  • Item must not have been used, washed, or altered.
  • Proof of purchase (payment receipt) must be provided.

How to Request a Refund:

Please use the "Request Refund" button in the top dashboard. Fill out the form with your Order ID, full name, full address, account details, and upload a copy of your payment receipt and valid ID. Our team will review your request within 3-5 business days.

Refund Processing:

Once approved, refunds will be processed to the bank account provided within 5-7 business days. If you paid via Paystack, the refund may be returned to your original payment method.

Non-Refundable Items:

Gift cards, downloadable software, and intimate apparel are non-refundable.

For any questions, please contact our support team at 08024300891.
