Refund Policy

At SwiftSelect, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, you may request a refund within 14 days of receiving your order.

Eligibility for Refunds:

  • Item must be defective, damaged, or significantly different from the description.
  • Item must not have been used, washed, or altered.
  • Proof of purchase must be provided.

To request a refund, please use the "Request Refund / Complaint" button in the footer of our website.

Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to the provided bank account.
